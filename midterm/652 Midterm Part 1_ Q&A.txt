10.  Why do we use media queries?
A media query allows you to change the format of your content if the webpage is smaller than a certain amount of pixals. If a person makes their window smaller than a given amount, the layout of the page will change to accommodate that. 

11. What is the main difference between a serif and a sans-serif font?
Sans-serif fonts are more clean and don't have the extra details that a serif font has. Sans-serif fonts are a bit more modern looking, while serif fonts look more traditional.
